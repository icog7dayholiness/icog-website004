The Crucifixion and Resurrection Timeline

A biblical examination of the Wednesday crucifixion, the two Sabbaths that week, the three days and three nights, and the timing of Christ’s resurrection.

This study follows the chronology from Christ’s death and burial before sunset on Wednesday through the discovery of the empty tomb on the first day of the week.

About This Study

The presentation examines biblical evening-to-evening day counting, the distinction between the high Sabbath and the weekly Sabbath, the three-days-and-three-nights chronology, and the discovery of the empty tomb.
